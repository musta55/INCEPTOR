Pick a run in the matrix. The playhead steps through its actions and every view follows it; click any action (a step row, a timeline mark, a fingerprint bubble) to jump there.

Trajectory: phases → subphases → steps. A subphase is a stretch of one phase with one outcome (✓ succeeded, ✗ failed, ⊘ blocked, ○ unchecked), with the evidence behind it (test, build, patch files). The dashed line marks the first fix: the first edit of app source. ⟲ marks repair attempts: edits up to the run that judges them.

Links keep the selection: bug.html#bug=8&t=codex-r2&i=5. Add &play=1 to start playing on open, &speed=8 for the speed, &details=0 to keep the details closed, &v=fingerprint&fp=runs for the fingerprint of one agent's runs.
