Bugs / Statistics

Each agent cell: run-1 outcome (✓ / ✗), a strip of the run's phases with its subphases' outcomes under it (length = number of actions), the action count, and the outcome of runs 1–3 (filled = pass, ring = fail). Click a bug to open its page with all 3 runs.

Phases: understanding, localization, reproduction, repair, validation. A subphase is a stretch of one phase with one outcome: ✓ succeeded, ✗ failed, ⊘ blocked (the environment stopped the check), ○ unchecked (no check). The words follow the annotation codebook.
